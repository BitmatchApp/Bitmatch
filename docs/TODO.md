## BitMatch Remote proposal

Remote is a proposed fourth mobile mode, after transfer reliability and evidence work. Keep the existing iPhone/iPad Copy & Verify, Check and Master Report modes fully functional, including standalone USB-C offload. The Mac menu bar companion is implemented; its remaining native checks are listed above. Remote starts read-only and monitors a Mac that remains sole authority for storage, queue, transfers, verification, journal, reports and verdicts. Reuse the shared coordinator, rules and presentation models; no second engine, UI-click simulation, helper or daemon. Mac hosting stays free and open source, with no accounts, subscriptions or internet requirement. **$5.99 lifetime** is a pricing hypothesis only, not a decision. Physical iOS local offload is separate from Remote and from later TestFlight/App Store distribution.

1. [ ] **Read-only monitoring.** Use Bonjour discovery and authenticated encrypted local-network pairing, with explicit Mac opt-in, one-time code/QR, persistent device identity, connected-client visibility and revocation. Use an explicit privacy-safe station alias instead of silently exporting the Mac network name; minimize names/paths and consider display-only privacy. Send an authoritative initial snapshot, bounded/coalesced updates and paginated on-demand history, bound to host/session identity and revisions. Reconnect fetches fresh state; never attach a cached safe verdict to a new card. Keep connection freshness—live, delayed or unknown—separate from transfer phase and show the actual operator-needed reason. Stale 100% and timeouts never imply completion. Client disconnect/background suspension does not affect Mac work; Mac sleep/quit makes the view stale. No unauthenticated controls or automatic diagnostic/report uploads. Handle networks that block discovery or peer traffic.
2. [ ] **Setup, queue and Start.** Show Mac-attached sources, authorized destinations, capacity and Mac readiness. Commands use opaque host-validated identities, current operation/queue revisions and stable idempotent request IDs. The Mac obtains any needed folder permission and revalidates source, destination and device identity at execution. Preserve preflight, no-overwrite and independence rules. Disconnected clients cannot submit commands.
3. [ ] **Controls and outcomes.** Add pause/resume, retry, queue management and confirmed cancellation. Show engine stages, per-destination progress, measured throughput/ETA when available, warnings and authoritative outcomes. Reject or reconcile stale mutations; retries retain the prior attempt and cannot bypass Mac rules.
4. [ ] **Connectivity and release gates.** Core support is Wi-Fi and wired Ethernet. Test powered USB-C Ethernet hubs and network changes. Prove direct USB app networking with public APIs before promising it; cable attachment alone is not proof. Bluetooth is deferred. Validate disconnect/restart/suspension, missed events, duplicate and out-of-order commands, stale/replaced media, failed preflight, overlap/overwrite attempts, revoked clients, cancel/retry, bounded event queues, large history, sandbox/file-provider limits, local-network declarations, layouts and physical-device background limits.
