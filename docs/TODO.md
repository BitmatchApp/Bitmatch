# To do

Reviewed October 20, 2026. This is a list of bounded roadmap candidates, not a promised release scope. **0.2.10 is public; 0.2.11 is back in draft at the user's request.** Nothing here authorizes publication. Record shipped work in the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md) and retain its evidence in the linked validation records.

**Source media → trustworthy copies → independent verification → accurate evidence → safe-to-erase decision.** The card stays untouched, destinations are never overwritten, and incomplete or unverified work never looks complete. Mac, iPad and iPhone share the same rules; platform limits stay explicit. See the [thesis](THESIS.md), [architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md), and current [release record](validation/RELEASE_0_2_11.md).

Engine, Mac, simulator, signed-app, synthetic and physical results are distinct evidence. GitHub Actions CI remains intentionally disabled. Native Dock behavior was not observed; physical iOS, real-device VoiceOver and power-loss behavior remain unproven. Field reports #10 and #23 are still open, and no fix is attributed to them without evidence.

- [ ] **Unknown physical-drive identity.** The current policy counts unresolved destinations separately pending positive evidence of a shared disk. Setup now distinguishes resolved identity groups from unknown destinations without changing verdicts or saved counts. Decide how Setup, staged queues, completion, safe-to-erase wording, History and reports describe verified copies versus *known* independent devices. Cover shared, distinct, unresolved, RAID/composite, network and mobile-provider cases. Do not silently demote mobile file-provider destinations or present unknown identity as proven independence; scheduling must follow the same evidence limit.
- [ ] **Output ownership and recovery.** A failed readback currently leaves the copied file at its normal name, although the verdict fails closed. Marking or moving it must prove this attempt owns the unchanged file; read errors do not prove corruption. Inject mismatches, read errors, replacement, interruption and marking failure; never overwrite, delete or relabel another writer's file. Show the real path and uncertainty in History/reports. A one-time re-copy/re-verify has the same ownership requirement, preserves the initial failure and retry outcome, and stays unsafe if retry fails. Repair from a verified sibling depends on recovery provenance: revalidate the sibling and independently verify the repaired destination, retain source/repair/new-attempt evidence, and reject stale evidence or same-device copies.
- [ ] **Transient source faults and card health.** Model source changes and read instability separately from destination failures. Distinguish “media verified; requested MHL/report could not be saved” from a media mismatch, and reveal existing evidence or offer an authorized export location. Any later handoff-only retry must revalidate backing media and publish new evidence without overwriting the old attempt.
- [ ] **Per-destination out-of-space handling.** Consider pausing one failed destination while others finish, then revalidate space and identity before resuming. Partial work remains incomplete, cancellation remains available, and no overall safe verdict is possible until every required backup verifies.
- [ ] **Finer reused-file progress.** Byte-weighted transfer and queue progress is implemented. Estimates remain separate from verification evidence. See the [progress validation](validation/RELEASE_0_2_11.md).

Keep source reread, local backup verification, SFTP upload and remote readback as separate facts: an SFTP upload-only result is “Uploaded · Unverified,” and remote readback cannot establish source assurance. Keep narrow-device wording and transient-source, repeatably wrong-source and destination-fault regressions current.

## Evidence and History

- [ ] **Upstream media provenance.** Check can read ASC inventories and standalone classic MHL 1.1 content evidence. A future source check should report “Matches the supplied manifest” within explicit digest/history/authenticity limits. An unsigned manifest cannot prove the camera's original correctness; an older BitMatch record can show unchanged-since-recorded content but is not independent pre-arrival provenance. Reuse source-read digests only after proving identity and unchanged ordering. Malformed, incomplete, mismatched or unsupported evidence gets a separate honest verdict and never blocks preserving damaged-but-irreplaceable media.
- [ ] **ASC MHL structure and history extension.** The implemented read-only slice checks inventory digests, ordered generations, safe paths, exact coverage and unchanged evidence during Check. Add supported root/directory hashes and rename/move lineage before extending histories. Keep file contents, chain records and directory structure as distinct scopes; unsupported records stay explicit. A valid chain does not authenticate custody or prove an unanchored historical tail was never removed. Continue refusing to write a new history generation until interruption cannot expose a partial generation as complete and independent validation succeeds. Preserve existing histories.
- [ ] **History imported from drives.** Read saved reports from connected, authorized destinations incrementally and within bounds, so a shuttle filled on another Mac can show prior offloads. Deduplicate attempts; treat malformed/partial records as untrusted. Imported evidence is a record of a previous result, never fresh verification or an automatic safe-to-erase verdict. Keep large drives and disconnect/reconnect responsive; do not scan an entire NAS whenever History opens.
- [ ] **Portable workflow settings.** Versioned Settings export/import already covers verification, ASC MHL, global AppleDouble, report/thumbnail switches and basic naming. It previews changes, rejects unsupported/oversized files and blocks Apply during active, queued or prepared work. Remaining design: portable folder recipes and reusable destination configuration. Never serialize existing job/preset records, credentials, bookmarks, paths, identities, history or private report fields, and never activate imported destinations automatically. Revalidate destinations on the receiving machine.
- [ ] **History memory and persistence.** After the actual-app measurements above, design bounded load/persistence if evidence supports it. Startup already skips unchanged journal rewrites; mutations still encode saved results, and the app loads the journal on the main actor. Any paging/archive boundary must preserve IDs, outcomes, retries, exports, legacy ID migration and interrupted recovery; malformed records stay fail-closed and derived caches invalidate on evidence revisions. On-demand History search and cached completed-card summaries are implemented; their scope and limits are linked in the [search probe](validation/history-search-2026-10-20.md), [journal probe](validation/journal-startup-2026-10-20.md), and [queue summary probe](validation/queue-summaries-2026-10-20.md).

## BitMatch Remote proposal

Remote is a proposed fourth mobile mode, after transfer reliability and evidence work. Keep the existing iPhone/iPad Copy & Verify, Check and Master Report modes fully functional, including standalone USB-C offload. The Mac menu bar companion is implemented; its remaining native checks are listed above. Remote starts read-only and monitors a Mac that remains sole authority for storage, queue, transfers, verification, journal, reports and verdicts. Reuse the shared coordinator, rules and presentation models; no second engine, UI-click simulation, helper or daemon. Mac hosting stays free and open source, with no accounts, subscriptions or internet requirement. **$5.99 lifetime** is a pricing hypothesis only, not a decision. Physical iOS local offload is separate from Remote and from later TestFlight/App Store distribution.

1. [ ] **Read-only monitoring.** Use Bonjour discovery and authenticated encrypted local-network pairing, with explicit Mac opt-in, one-time code/QR, persistent device identity, connected-client visibility and revocation. Use an explicit privacy-safe station alias instead of silently exporting the Mac network name; minimize names/paths and consider display-only privacy. Send an authoritative initial snapshot, bounded/coalesced updates and paginated on-demand history, bound to host/session identity and revisions. Reconnect fetches fresh state; never attach a cached safe verdict to a new card. Keep connection freshness—live, delayed or unknown—separate from transfer phase and show the actual operator-needed reason. Stale 100% and timeouts never imply completion. Client disconnect/background suspension does not affect Mac work; Mac sleep/quit makes the view stale. No unauthenticated controls or automatic diagnostic/report uploads. Handle networks that block discovery or peer traffic.
2. [ ] **Setup, queue and Start.** Show Mac-attached sources, authorized destinations, capacity and Mac readiness. Commands use opaque host-validated identities, current operation/queue revisions and stable idempotent request IDs. The Mac obtains any needed folder permission and revalidates source, destination and device identity at execution. Preserve preflight, no-overwrite and independence rules. Disconnected clients cannot submit commands.
3. [ ] **Controls and outcomes.** Add pause/resume, retry, queue management and confirmed cancellation. Show engine stages, per-destination progress, measured throughput/ETA when available, warnings and authoritative outcomes. Reject or reconcile stale mutations; retries retain the prior attempt and cannot bypass Mac rules.
4. [ ] **Connectivity and release gates.** Core support is Wi-Fi and wired Ethernet. Test powered USB-C Ethernet hubs and network changes. Prove direct USB app networking with public APIs before promising it; cable attachment alone is not proof. Bluetooth is deferred. Validate disconnect/restart/suspension, missed events, duplicate and out-of-order commands, stale/replaced media, failed preflight, overlap/overwrite attempts, revoked clients, cancel/retry, bounded event queues, large history, sandbox/file-provider limits, local-network declarations, layouts and physical-device background limits. Synthetic, simulator and device evidence stay distinct. No screen sharing, filesystem browsing, shell, cloud relay, automatic eject/format, facility management or multi-host coordination.
