## Disposition

The 20,000-file candidate rerun and synthetic PDF scale probe are encouraging controlled results, but they do not reproduce issue #23, establish a general bound for complex reports, or complete platform validation. The reporter's version and failing phase remain unknown. Keep issue #23 open and PRs #79, #80, and #81 held pending the remaining workload and review checks.
