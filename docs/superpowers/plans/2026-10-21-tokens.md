## What ships

1. `DesignTokens.swift`: the spacing scale (4/8/12/16/24/32, gutter and
   section 24, container padding 16, row padding 12, control heights
   28/32, bar 56) and radii (12 containers, 8 controls, capsule pills,
   nested = outer − padding floored at 4), applied to the custom surfaces
   touched by this spec (Setup wells and panels, queue rows, action bar).
   System Forms and sheets keep their own metrics; the PDF report layout
   and the Dock tile keep their drawn sizes.
2. Type: text styles only, two weights (regular and semibold). Row and
   well titles `.headline`; body and buttons `.body`; meta and hints
   `.callout`, never smaller; state labels `.subheadline` semibold;
   numbers keep `monospacedDigit`. Hand-picked `.system(size:)` calls on
   the interactive surfaces (queue headline, toast, setup icons) become
   text styles. InterfaceLab and Photographer keep theirs (spec §6
   builder decision, same boundary).
3. Colour: text stays `.primary/.secondary/.tertiary`. `CardSafetyTint`
   and `TransferPlanStatusTone` gain iOS light values that clear 4.5:1 on
   white (safe pill #1b7f37 with white text) and Increase Contrast
   variants: opaque fills, a 1 pt border, and the attention edge widening
   from 4 pt to 6 pt. The Mac is forced dark, so light values matter only
   on iOS. Contrast ratios are pinned by a unit test.
4. Validate under the test lock (Mac suite, iPad build) and live-check
   the Mac surfaces; iOS light/Increase Contrast need a simulator or
   device pass by a human and are named in the PR.
