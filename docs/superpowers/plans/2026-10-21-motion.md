# Step 6: motion — plan

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-visual-polish-design.md` §6.
Branch `feat/motion` stacked on `feat/setup-hierarchy` (#88); retarget to
main once that merges. Inline execution in Muse; one PR.

## What ships

1. `Motion` tokens (`Shared/Views/Motion.swift`): settle (spring
   0.42/bounce 0.14), quick (easeOut 200 ms), commit (the 560 ms
   choreography constants: flash 200 ms, ring 240 ms from +80 ms, check and
   Eject at +280 ms, flash settle 600 ms, stagger 120 ms capped at three),
   data (linear 300 ms, nil under Reduce Motion), attention (150 ms).
   Native animations (segmented thumb, sheets, disclosure) stay native.
2. The six existing call sites route through the tokens: queue list spring,
   the three expand/collapse durations, the hero ring, and the composer
   flight (Mac shell and the shared locations transition).
3. `RowProgressFill`: a leaf view taking only its own fraction (Equatable,
   so unchanged rows do not redraw), 22 % accent fill (12 % with a 1 pt
   border in iOS light), 1.5 pt leading edge, no glow, 2 pt bottom track,
   `drawingGroup` on the fill. While verifying, one lighter layer and a
   paler track. It replaces the stock `ProgressView` in the collapsed row
   and stays on the header strip when expanded. New rows start at zero.
4. Commit: only when the row is on screen and the app is frontmost
   (`scenePhase == .active`). The row fill flashes green over 200 ms and
   settles over 600 ms while the hero ring draws on the commit beats;
   several finishes stagger by 120 ms, capped at three. Eject is enabled
   the instant the result commits — no lockout is added. Reduce Motion
   keeps the existing pre-drawn ring and crossfades.
5. Attention: no sweep. The edge grows over 150 ms and the row tint
   desaturates over 150 ms; no wiggle.
6. Responsive delight where it is cheap and safe: row hover brightens the
   surface 3 % (Mac, no lift), tab/page changes crossfade through
   `Motion.quick`. Nothing moves on its own; no sound.
